The principles that power every product

Safer

More than 120 of our products are certified through the U.S. EPA Safer Choice program. That means every formula is evaluated for human and environmental safety. We’ve received the Safer Choice Partner of the Year award eight times, including in 2024.

Learn More

Sustainable

We’ve been carbon neutral since 2013 and water neutral since 2016. Our manufacturing facilities are TRUE Platinum Zero Waste certified, and we operate as a climate positive company. Every formula is vegan and never tested on animals.

Learn More

Affordable

We believe everyone deserves access to safer cleaning. As a family-owned brand since 1967, we’re committed to making wellness-forward, science-backed products that fit real household budgets.
